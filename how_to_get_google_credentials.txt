HOW TO FIND GOOGLE CLIENT ID & SECRET
=====================================

1. Go to Google Cloud Console
   Visit: https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/credentials

2. Select Your Project
   Make sure the correct project (e.g., "CodeMaster AI") is selected in the top dropdown bar.

3. Find the "OAuth 2.0 Client IDs" Section
   - Look for a table row under "OAuth 2.0 Client IDs".
   - You should see the credential you created/used before (e.g., "Web client 1").
   - Click the **Pencil Icon** (Edit) or the **Name** of the client.

4. Copy the Values
   On the right side of the screen (or top), you will see:
   - **Client ID**: Ends in `...apps.googleusercontent.com`
   - **Client secret**: A string of random characters (click the "copy" icon).

   -> These are the two values you need to paste into Vercel!
