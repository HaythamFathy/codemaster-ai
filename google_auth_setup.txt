GOOGLE AUTH SETUP FOR VERCEL
============================

1. Go to Google Cloud Console
-----------------------------
1. Visit https://console.cloud.google.com/
2. Select your project (the one you used for localhost development).

2. Update Credentials
---------------------
1. Go to "APIs & Services" -> "Credentials".
2. Click on your existing "OAuth 2.0 Client ID" (or create a new one).

3. Configure URLs
-----------------
You must add your Vercel URLs here.

A) **Authorized JavaScript Origins**
   Add your Vercel production URL:
   - `https://your-project-name.vercel.app`

B) **Authorized Redirect URIs**
   Add the callback URL. **Critical:** Use the `/api` prefix because the backend handles this.
   - `https://your-project-name.vercel.app/api/auth/google/callback`

   *Note: If you have a custom domain, add that too.*

4. Configure Vercel Environment Variables
-----------------------------------------
Go to your Vercel Project Settings -> Environment Variables.
Ensure you have:

   Key                   Value
   ---                   -----
   GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID      (From Google Console)
   GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET  (From Google Console)
   BACKEND_URL           https://your-project-name.vercel.app/api
   FRONTEND_URL          https://your-project-name.vercel.app

   *Note: BACKEND_URL must include `/api` at the end.*

5. Redeploy
-----------
You need to redeploy for the Vercel Environment Variables to take effect (or just hit "Redeploy" on the dashboard).

6. Verify
---------
- Go to your site.
- Click "Sign in with Google".
- It should redirect to Google, then back to your site, and log you in.
